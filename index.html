ShadowFix: Deep-Learning Shadow Removal

Restores what's hidden under real-world shadows, deployed as a Gradio app.

  • Trained 3 U-Net variants (RGB, residual, mask-guided; ~7.8M params) on ISTD with mixed precision on an NVIDIA T4.
  • Split PSNR by region to show residual models over-correct non-shadow pixels (−1.3 dB), which a single score hides.
  • Gradio app with automatic (OpenCV LAB-space mask) and paint-guided restoration.
