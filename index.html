View repository ↗Technical case study ↓
ShadowFix — approach, evaluation & limitations

The approach

Train U-Net-style encoder–decoder models on paired ISTD images at 256 × 256 resolution. Compare RGB baselines with residual and mask-guided variants; evaluate the full image and shadow region separately.

The evidence

Reported mask-guided results: 30.189 dB PSNR and 0.9514 SSIM across 540 test images, versus 27.176 dB and 0.9324 for the RGB baseline. These are capstone results documented in the repository, not a fresh benchmark rerun.

The tradeoffs

Unfamiliar scenes can retain shadow traces or introduce washed-out dark areas, halos, and color shifts. The notebook requires separately supplied data, checkpoints, and project files to run.
